Join Us

ASPIRE Lab at Indiana University Bloomington is recruiting motivated Ph.D. students and research interns interested in trustworthy AI, speech and acoustic security, wireless sensing security, and agent security.

How to apply

Current IU students

If you are a current IU student (undergraduate or graduate) and want to join the lab, email guohan@iu.edu with:

  • Your CV
  • A short note on your research interests / topic of interest

Please use a subject line such as “ASPIRE IU Student – [Your Name]”.

Ph.D. applicants

If you are applying for a Ph.D. position, email guohan@iu.edu with:

  • Your CV
  • Transcripts
  • A brief description of your research interests and how they connect to ASPIRE’s thrusts
  • English proficiency score: TOEFL, IELTS, or Duolingo (if required)

Please use a subject line such as “ASPIRE PhD Application – [Your Name]”. Admission requirements and program details are on the Luddy Graduate Admissions page.
