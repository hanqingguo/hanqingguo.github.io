ASPIRE Lab

Acoustic Security Protecting Intelligent Real Environments

We secure intelligent systems that interact with people, communicate through voice, sense the physical world, and act autonomously.

Join Us Explore Research

Research Thrusts

Human–Mobile

Secure people ↔ devices: voice assistants, speaker verification, and mobile sensing under attack.

Human–Human

Secure people ↔ people: stop deepfakes, scam calls, hidden recording, and dataset misuse.

Human–Agent

Secure people ↔ AI agents: jailbreaks, scraper abuse, and proving a human is really there.
